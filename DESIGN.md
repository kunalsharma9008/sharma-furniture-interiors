## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an Architectural Digest-level editorial presence tailored for an artisanal, high-end interior architecture and bespoke furniture studio in Pune, Maharashtra. The visual identity evokes an emotional atmosphere of quiet luxury, meticulous joinery, tactile material honesty, and serene spatial grandeur.

The system merges **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Architectural Structure**:
- **Generous Spatial Breathing Room:** Expansive ivory margins mimic gallery walls, framing custom furniture compositions and architectural plans without visual clutter.
- **Craftsmanship & Materiality:** Subtle shifts between linen ivory, honed travertine, raw sand, and oiled teak convey the tangible reality of physical stone, hand-rubbed timber, and patinated brass.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke heritage monographs and bespoke design publications, grounded by razor-sharp, geometric UI typography that guarantees contemporary digital utility.
- **Quiet Micro-Interactions:** Restrained motion with ease curves inspired by soft-close luxury cabinetry—smooth, weighted, and deliberate.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from natural luxury finishes: warm sage flora, hand-oiled teakwood, polished champagne brass, sun-bleached ivory plaster, and deep charred umber.

### Color Roles & Application

- **Background & Canvas:**
  - `Canvas Main (#FAF7F1)`: The radiant, warm ivory foundational surface. Never stark clinical white.
  - `Surface Subtle (#F5F1E9)`: Soft alabaster for nested sections, editorial quotes, and backdrop bands.
  - `Surface Elevated (#FFFFFF)`: Crisp off-white card faces and modal containers, designed to sit proud against ivory canvases.
  - `Surface Recessed (#E9DFD0)`: Natural sand tint for architectural metadata blocks, spec pill tags, and swatch wells.

- **Primary Accent (`#6C8264`):**
  - Muted Indian Sage represents organic balance, botanical courtyards, and artisanal finishes. Used for primary interactive triggers, focal badge highlights, key selections, and featured portfolio tags.
  - `Sage Tint (#B9C9B1)`: Subdued secondary highlight for hover fills, progress fills, and active category backdrops.

- **Secondary Accent (`#C5A47E`):**
  - Warm Teak & Champagne Brass tone. Reserved for luxury metadata (e.g., custom wood finishes, artisan markers, hairline accents, and curated project tags).

- **Neutral & Typographic Spectrum:**
  - `Text Primary (#23201D)`: Charred Umber; delivers high-contrast readability with rich warmth, avoiding flat digital black.
  - `Text Muted (#443E38)`: Muted Espresso for secondary paragraphs, deck descriptions, and table headers.
  - `Text Tertiary (#766E65)`: Warm gray for captions, dimension units, and breadcrumb trails.

- **Architectural Borders & Rules:**
  - `Hairline Border (#E4DACB)`: Champagne wood tone used for 1px hairline dividers, card bounding boxes, and architectural grid guides.

## Typography

The typography pairs the poise of classical architectural monographs with modern digital legibility.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (Playfair Display):** Set with deliberate negative tracking and generous line heights to preserve the elegance of high-contrast serif ligatures. Italics are reserved for artisanal quotes, provenance details, and project location callouts (e.g., *Koregaon Park, Pune*).
- **Body & Micro-Interactions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A geometric sans-serif that balances modernism with organic warmth. Generous x-height ensures crisp legibility for dimensions, material specifications, and design notes.
- **Labels, Overlines & Metas:** Always set in uppercase with relaxed letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to create an authentic architectural blueprint feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column responsive fluid grid system designed to frame visual assets as high-value gallery pieces.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Structural Flow
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns with `4rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Editorial hero modules and full-bleed bespoke interior spreads break outside the grid dynamically while keeping copy anchored to an 8-column reading spine.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Two-up gallery presentations and stacked floor plans.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-column specs collapse into structured horizontal swipe strips and vertical detail sheets.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing is rhythmically scaled using base-8 multiples, prioritizing generous vertical relief (`space-xl` and higher multiples) between sections to maintain the luxurious pacing of physical print folios.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid synthetic, glassy software tropes, depth is defined by **tactile layering**, **honed surface transitions**, and **ambient natural light casting**.

- **Surface Layering:** Contrast is established by stacking `Surface Elevated (#FFFFFF)` or `Surface Subtle (#F5F1E9)` against the primary `Canvas Main (#FAF7F1)` rather than using heavy shadows.
- **Ambient Sun-Cast Shadows:** Shadows reflect soft, diffused daylight pouring into an open courtyard. They carry an umber tint rather than cold neutral gray:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 2px 12px -2px rgba(35, 32, 29, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(35, 32, 29, 0.03)`
  - *Floating / Hover Elevation:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(35, 32, 29, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(35, 32, 29, 0.03)`
  - *Dialog / Drawer Modal:* `0 24px 54px -8px rgba(35, 32, 29, 0.12), 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(35, 32, 29, 0.04)`
- **Hairline Bounding:** Floating containers, gallery frames, and structural cards are framed by a 1px solid hairline in `Subtle Champagne Wood (#E4DACB)` to emulate physical shadow gaps found in bespoke joinery.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is informed by precision architectural craftsmanship, dressed stone, and hand-planed hardwood edges.

- **Level 1 (Soft):** 
  - Standard interactive elements (buttons, form inputs, spec tags) carry a modest `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, providing a refined, eased finish reminiscent of softened wood corners.
  - Cards, modal viewports, and imagery containers employ `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- **Proportional Geometry:** Images and interior mockups default to architectural aspect ratios: 4:5 vertical editorial portrait, 16:10 wide showcase, and 1:1 material swatch circles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep charred umber background (`#23201D`) or warm muted sage (`#6C8264`) with crisp off-white text (`#FFFFFF`). Padding: `14px 28px`. Typography: `label-md` with uppercase letter spacing. Radius: `4px`. Subtle hover state deepens saturation by 4% with a `0.2s ease` transition.
- **Secondary / Architectural Ghost:** Ivory or transparent background with a 1px solid hairline border in `#E4DACB`. Text color: `#23201D`. On hover: surface transitions to `#F5F1E9` and border shifts to `#C5A47E`.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Unbordered, featuring an understated brass-toned (`#C5A47E`) underline offset by 4px that smoothly expands on hover.

### Cards & Portfolio Showcases
- **Project Showcase Card:** White surface (`#FFFFFF`) bound by a 1px `#E4DACB` hairline. Generous padding (`24px`). Features an image container with a 4:5 ratio, headline in `Playfair Display`, and an architectural spec footer displaying timber species, square footage, and neighborhood in `label-sm`.
- **Material & Swatch Card:** Sand tinted background (`#E9DFD0`) with high-resolution circular texture preview (e.g., Fluted Teak, Calacatta Gold Marble, Raw Linen). Subdued label beneath with finish ID and tactile grade.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Background set to `#FFFFFF` against canvas. 1px solid border in `#E4DACB`. Padding: `14px 16px`. Active / Focus state introduces a clean 1px outline in `#6C8264` without an aggressive outer glow. Placeholder text in `#766E65`.
- **Floating Labels:** Form labels employ `label-sm` in charred umber (`#23201D`), set strictly above input fields for an uncluttered, architectural blueprint layout.

### Chips & Tags
- **Specification Chips:** Sand-toned container (`#E9DFD0`) with charred umber text (`#23201D`), rendered in `label-sm` uppercase. Height: `28px`. Used for categorization such as "SOLID BURMA TEAK", "MODULAR PANTRY", or "BRASS INLAY".
- **Selected Status Chip:** Warm muted sage background (`#6C8264`) with off-white text (`#FFFFFF`).

### Lists & Spec Grids
- **Architectural Spec Rows:** Alternating data presentation using full-width 1px `#E4DACB` border dividers. Left column holds the property attribute (e.g., "Hardware", "Carcass Core", "Finish Polish") in `title-md`, right column details the specification in `body-md` espresso (`#443E38`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Interactive Checkboxes:** 18x18px square with 2px corner radius. Inactive border: 1.5px `#E4DACB`. Checked state fills with `#6C8264` accompanied by an ivory check icon.
- **Custom Finish Selectors (Radio):** Visual swatch buttons with a 1px border that expands to a 2px dual-ring outline in `#C5A47E` when active.
